Information the Application stores on your device

The Application stores the following data locally on your device only, using the device’s built-in storage (AsyncStorage). None of this data is transmitted to or stored on any server operated by the Service Provider, who has no backend infrastructure and no remote database that would receive it.

  • A display name that you freely choose during account creation. A pseudonym is acceptable; you are not required to enter your real name.
  • Your learning progress (lessons completed, XP earned, virtual currency balance, achievements unlocked, daily streak, language preferences).
  • Your app settings (selected interface language, theme, motion + haptic preferences, selected avatar).
  • Up to a small number of locally saved account snapshots if you choose to switch between multiple in-app profiles on the same device.

Because this data lives only on your device, the Service Provider cannot retrieve, restore, export, or delete it for you. Uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage removes all of it from your device permanently.
